##Project Overview
# Tawredaa (توريدة) - B2B Mobile Distribution Platform

A dedicated B2B distribution and commerce mobile application bridging manufacturers and FMCG wholesalers with small-to-medium retail outlets—including cosmetics shops, pharmacies, and detergent retailers.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To digitize and streamline traditional retail supply chains by providing merchants with frictionless ordering, transparent wholesale pricing, and reliable doorstep delivery—empowering local retail businesses to scale efficiently while eliminating inventory stockouts.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Tawredaa** is a mobile-first B2B distribution solution designed to address inefficiencies in traditional FMCG and specialty retail supply chains. By replacing fragmented ordering via field reps and phone calls with a consolidated digital marketplace, Tawredaa enables retail store owners to:

- Browse multi-category wholesale catalogs directly from verified suppliers.
- Consolidate purchase orders across multiple brands into a single shipment.
- Track deliveries, view credit terms, and manage replenishment schedules seamlessly.

---

## 🔭 Project Scope

### In Scope
- **Merchant Ordering App (iOS & Android):** Intuitive catalog navigation, batch quantity pricing, search, checkout, and order status tracking.
- **Supplier & Admin Dashboard:** Product listing, bulk pricing rules, inventory allocation, and fulfillment monitoring.
- **Delivery & Fleet Module:** Order dispatching, delivery verification (digital proof of delivery), and basic routing.
- **Core Retail Verticals:** Initial product categories strictly focused on:
  - Cosmetics & Personal Care
  - Pharmaceuticals & Over-The-Counter (OTC) Health Supplies
  - Detergents, Household Cleaning & Paper Goods

### Out of Scope (Initial Phase)
- Cross-border wholesale shipping (localized domestic operations only).
- B2C direct-to-consumer sales.
- Third-party independent carrier marketplace bidding.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### For Retail Merchants (Mobile App)
- **Wholesale Multi-Brand Catalog:** Search and filter by category (Cosmetics, Pharma, Detergents), sub-brands, expiration dates, and packaging units (carton, pack, piece).
- **Tiered Volume Pricing:** Dynamic discounts based on minimum order quantities (MOQ) and batch volumes.
- **One-Tap Reordering:** Fast replenishment based on frequent purchase history and past invoices.
- **Flexible Settlement Options:** Cash on Delivery (COD), mobile wallets, and digital credit terms for vetted retailers.
- **Order Tracking & Notifications:** Real-time visibility from warehouse dispatch to retail shelf delivery.

### For Warehouse & Operations
- **Centralized Inventory Sync:** Real-time SKU tracking across warehouses to prevent overselling.
- **Digital Invoicing & Tax Receipts:** Compliant localized invoice generation attached to every delivery run.
- **Route Dispatch Integration:** Grouping orders by district and store cluster for optimal drop-off routes.
