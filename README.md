# Tawredaa – Company Profile & Digital Transformation Project

## 🎯 Company Mission

To improve and modernize traditional retail supply chains by providing merchants with convenient digital ordering, competitive wholesale prices, and reliable doorstep delivery. Tawredaa aims to simplify the purchasing process for retail businesses, improve product availability, and help local merchants manage their daily supply needs more efficiently.

---

## 📌 Company Overview

**Tawredaa** is an established B2B distribution company specializing in supplying and delivering a wide range of products to retail businesses, including cosmetics shops, pharmacies, and detergent stores.

The company operates within the wholesale distribution sector, connecting retail merchants with a diverse selection of products and brands through an organized supply and delivery service.

Tawredaa initially established its online presence through its official Facebook page, using social media as a communication and marketing channel to reach merchants, showcase available products, and receive customer inquiries and orders.

As part of its digital development, the company has also introduced a mobile application to make the ordering process more convenient and accessible, allowing merchants to interact with the company's services through digital channels.

Through its combination of product variety, wholesale distribution, and delivery services, Tawredaa seeks to strengthen its position in the local B2B market and improve the overall purchasing experience for retail merchants.

### Business Model

Tawredaa operates under a **Business-to-Business (B2B) business model**, focusing on supplying products to other businesses rather than selling directly to individual consumers.

Its primary customers include:

- Cosmetics and personal care stores.
- Pharmacies and healthcare retailers.
- Detergent and household cleaning stores.
- Retail businesses requiring paper products and general merchandise.

The company creates value by providing merchants with access to multiple product categories and brands through a single supply and delivery service.

## 🔭 Project Scope

This project focuses on analyzing and developing Tawredaa's existing business operations and digital presence, with the objective of improving its ordering experience, operational efficiency, and customer engagement.

### In Scope

#### 1. Digital Ordering Channels

- Improving the existing mobile application experience.
- Organizing product catalogs into clear categories and subcategories.
- Providing product search and filtering capabilities.
- Simplifying the ordering and checkout process.
- Improving order status visibility and customer notifications.

#### 2. Digital Marketing & Customer Acquisition

- Developing the company's Facebook presence as a marketing and communication channel.
- Creating promotional and educational content to attract retail merchants.
- Increasing brand awareness among the target audience.
- Encouraging merchants to download and use the mobile application.
- Developing strategies to increase customer retention and repeat orders.

#### 3. Product & Catalog Management

- Organizing products by category and brand.
- Displaying product descriptions, prices, and available quantities.
- Maintaining accurate product information.
- Highlighting promotions and wholesale offers.

#### 4. Order Management & Delivery Operations

- Improving the process of receiving and managing customer orders.
- Coordinating order preparation and delivery.
- Enhancing communication between the company and merchants.
- Improving delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.

#### 5. Core Business Categories

Tawredaa's product portfolio focuses on several retail categories, including:

- Cosmetics & Personal Care.
- Pharmaceutical & Healthcare Supplies.
- Detergents & Household Cleaning Products.
- Paper Products.
- Hardware & General Store Supplies.

### Out of Scope

The following activities are not included in the current project scope:

- International and cross-border distribution.
- Direct-to-consumer (B2C) sales.
- Establishing a third-party independent delivery marketplace.
- Expanding operations into international markets.
- Developing a completely new business model unrelated to the company's existing B2B operations.

## 🚀 Key Business Features & Services

### 1. For Retail Merchants

#### Wide Product Selection

Merchants can access a diverse range of products across multiple categories and brands, reducing the need to deal with multiple suppliers separately.

#### Convenient Ordering

The company provides digital ordering channels through its Facebook page and mobile application, making it easier for merchants to browse available products and place orders.

#### Competitive Wholesale Pricing

Tawredaa focuses on supplying products at wholesale prices suitable for retail businesses, helping merchants manage their purchasing costs.

#### Doorstep Delivery

The company provides delivery services directly to retail stores, reducing the time and effort merchants spend purchasing and transporting inventory.

#### Product Variety

Merchants can source different products and brands through one distribution company, making their purchasing process more convenient.

### 2. For Company Management & Operations

#### Order Management

Organizing incoming orders and coordinating their preparation and delivery to customers.

#### Product Management

Maintaining an organized product catalog that includes different brands, categories, and product information.

#### Inventory Coordination

Monitoring product availability and coordinating stock levels to support order fulfillment.

#### Delivery Coordination

Managing the delivery process to ensure that orders reach retail merchants efficiently and reliably.

#### Customer Relationship Management

Maintaining communication with merchants, responding to inquiries, and building long-term business relationships.

## 🗺️ Project Development Plan & Milestones

The following roadmap focuses on improving Tawredaa's existing digital infrastructure and business operations rather than establishing a new company or launching its services from scratch.

| Phase | Milestone | Focus Areas | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Business Analysis & Assessment | Analyzing the company's current business model, identifying target customers, evaluating existing digital channels, and identifying areas for improvement. | Weeks 1–3 |
| **Phase 2** | Digital Platform Optimization | Improving the existing mobile application, organizing product catalogs, enhancing navigation, and simplifying the ordering experience. | Weeks 4–8 |
| **Phase 3** | Marketing & Operational Enhancement | Developing social media marketing strategies, improving order management, strengthening customer communication, and optimizing delivery coordination. | Weeks 9–11 |
| **Phase 4** | Testing & Performance Evaluation | Evaluating the improvements, collecting customer feedback, identifying operational issues, and measuring digital platform performance. | Weeks 12–14 |
| **Phase 5** | Growth & Business Expansion | Increasing merchant acquisition, improving customer retention, expanding product availability, and strengthening Tawredaa's position in the local B2B distribution market. | Week 15+ |
